![](just-throw-away-line-63c7c11c.jpg)It's fascinating to me how serendipitous life often is. We just attended a week long World Religions Encounter, including hour upon hour of lectures and site visits for exposure to beliefs ranging from Wiccan to Zoroastriansim, and of all the things said and experienced there the thought that struck me most forcefully came from a casual comment made by a fellow conference-goer during a bus tour: "The larger our island of knowledge, the longer the shore of uncertainty."
