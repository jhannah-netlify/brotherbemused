I am reminded of the Seven Deadly Sins as I ponder our national politics at the
highest level. The list I reviewed to refresh my memory ordered them thusly:
Pride. Greed. Wrath. Envy. Lust. Gluttony. And, lastly, Sloth. The first six
brought to mind parallels of specific politicians and policies that seem to
have become acceptable—even ascendant—today. The resultant damage to our
democratic republic by “flooding the zone” with daily, almost hourly, assaults
on our national honor seems designed to overwhelm and control. I never thought
I’d champion one of the Seven Deadly Sins, but as I consider today’s political
scene I find myself yearning for MORE SLOTH, LESS SLEAZE.
